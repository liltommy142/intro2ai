
# FAI Project Guidelines (Rewritten)

## 1. Project Objective

Students will complete this project in the **same teams** that were formed for previous labs and project activities.

Each team must:

1. Explore recent research in the field of **Artificial Intelligence**.
2. Select **one research publication** that best matches the team's interests and capabilities.
3. Carefully study and understand the selected paper.
4. Produce an educational video in the style of **3Blue1Brown** that clearly explains the paper's main ideas and contributions.

The final video must **not exceed 30 minutes**.

---

## 2. Video Requirements

The video should follow the storytelling and visualization style of the **3Blue1Brown** YouTube channel.

Instead of simply presenting slides or reading the paper, the video should:

- Explain the problem in an intuitive way.
- Build understanding step by step.
- Use animations to illustrate concepts.
- Help viewers understand *why* the proposed method works.

Students are expected to use **Manim** to create these animations.

Useful resources:

- Official Manim documentation
- Manim GitHub repository
- Community tutorials and examples

**Important:**
The animations are created programmatically using Manim. They are **not AI-generated videos**, which is a common misconception.

---

## 3. AI Usage Policy

The use of AI tools is **allowed**.

However:

- AI should be used as a learning assistant rather than replacing your own understanding.
- Every team member should fully understand the selected paper.
- The lab instructor may conduct additional oral assessments or interviews if necessary to verify the team's understanding.

---

## 4. Publication Registration

Each team must register exactly **one publication**.

Before registering:

- Check the registration spreadsheet to ensure the paper has not already been selected by another team.
- If another team has already registered the same paper, you must choose a different one.

If your team submits the registration form multiple times:

- Only the **last submission** will be considered.
- Earlier submissions become invalid.
- You may lose your previously selected paper if another team registers it before your final submission.

Editing an existing submission is **not allowed**. Any modification requires submitting a **new registration form**.

---

## 5. Publication Selection Requirements

Your selected publication must satisfy the following conditions.

### 5.1 Publication Time

The paper must be **no older than three years**.

Rules:

- If only the publication year is available, acceptable years are:
  - Current year
  - Previous year
  - Two years before the current year

- If the publication month is available, the paper must have been published within **36 months** prior to registration (inclusive).

---

### 5.2 Publication Quality

The publication should appear in a **high-quality venue**.

Two types of venues are accepted:

#### Conferences

Conference rankings are determined using **CORE Ranking**.

Accepted ranks:

- A
- A*

---

#### Journals

Journal quality is determined using the **SCIE (Web of Science)** index.

Accepted category:

- Q1

---

### 5.3 Citation-Based Exception

If the publication:

- is not published in an accepted venue, or
- is available only as a preprint,

then it may still be accepted if it satisfies the citation requirement.

Required citations:

```text
Required Citations = Publication Age (months) × 2
```

Example:

- Publication age = 12 months
- Required citations = 24

Citation counts should be obtained from **Google Scholar**.

---

### 5.4 Topic Requirement

The selected publication must:

- Clearly belong to the field of Artificial Intelligence.
- Match the project theme specified by the lab instructor on Moodle.

There are **no additional restrictions**.

Teams are encouraged to choose papers that best match:

- their interests,
- technical background,
- research direction,
- programming skills.

The difficulty of the paper is **not** considered a selection criterion.

Survey papers are acceptable if they satisfy all other requirements.

---

## 6. Registration Form Information

Each team must provide the following information.

### Publication Title

The complete title of the selected paper.

---

### Publication Year

The official publication year.

If the submission year differs from the publication year, use the publication year.

---

### Publication Month

If available:

- Conference papers: use the month when the conference officially took place.
- Journal papers: use the month when the journal issue containing the paper was published.

If unavailable, leave this field blank.

---

### Publication Venue

Provide:

For conference papers:

- Full conference name
- Conference abbreviation
- Do **not** include the year

For journal papers:

- Journal name
- Publisher

For preprints:

- Name of the preprint server (e.g., arXiv, bioRxiv)

---

### Official Link

Provide the official publication page from the conference or journal website.

For preprints, use the official preprint page.

---

### Open-Access Link

If the official paper requires payment:

- Search for an official open-access version.
- Provide its link if available.

Otherwise, leave this field blank.

---

### Publication Quality

Provide one of:

- A
- A*
- Q1

If using the citation-based criterion, provide the current Google Scholar citation count instead.

---

### Proof of Quality

Provide the corresponding evidence link.

Examples include:

- CORE ranking page
- SCIE journal page
- Google Scholar citation page

---

## 7. Deliverables

Each team is expected to submit:

- A properly registered publication.
- A complete understanding of the selected paper.
- A 3Blue1Brown-style educational video created with Manim.
- A video with a maximum duration of **30 minutes**.

The instructor may additionally conduct interviews or oral assessments to verify the team's understanding.

Nếu muốn dùng tài liệu này làm **README của project GitHub** hoặc **Project Specification** cho nhóm A-Star, mình cũng có thể viết lại theo phong cách chuyên nghiệp hơn, giống tài liệu kỹ thuật của các nhóm nghiên cứu.
